# Task Node Privacy Policy

The hosted Task Node service at tasknode.postfiat.org is operated by
Chatterwise Limited, a company registered in the British Virgin Islands. All
data processed by the hosted service is subject to Chatterwise Limited.

The policy is published at <https://tasknode.postfiat.org/privacy> and its
source is [`docs/wiki/legal/privacy.md`](docs/wiki/legal/privacy.md), which is
the file to edit. A self-hosted fork is controlled by its own operator and must
publish its own policy.
