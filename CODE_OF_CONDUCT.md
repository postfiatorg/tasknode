# Community Code of Conduct

We want technical disagreement to be direct, evidence-based, and safe for
people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants must:

- criticize behavior, design, and evidence rather than attacking people;
- respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials, wallet
  secrets, private messages, personal data, or security report;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention,
  impersonation, and sustained disruption; and
- follow maintainer instructions during incidents and coordinated disclosure.

This applies in the repository, review systems, community support spaces, and
project events. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict access,
or permanently exclude participants when necessary to protect the community or
service.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact
or the official Post Fiat community contact. Security vulnerabilities must use
the process in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people
needed to investigate and respond; conflicts of interest will be routed to a
different maintainer.
